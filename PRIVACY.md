# Privacy policy

Before/After Compare does not collect, transmit or sell any data.

- It runs only on pages under `https://github.com/`, and only reads the screenshot tables already shown
  on the page in order to display them differently.
- It makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics or tracking.
- The only thing it stores is the comparison mode you last picked (for example "Slider"), in your
  browser's `localStorage` for github.com. It never leaves your browser.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
